Mountain Pass Realities & Seasonal Feeds

Real-time status notes and technical requirements for high mountain passes in Gilgit-Baltistan.

Seasonal Pass

Babusar Pass

4,173 meters (13,691 ft)

Open mid-June to late October only. Snowbound in winter; travelers must take KKH Kohistan route between Nov and May.

4WD Track Only

Fairy Meadows (Raikot)

3,300 meters (10,827 ft)

Narrow cliffside track from Raikot Bridge to Tattu village strictly requires union-registered local 4WD jeeps. Personal cars prohibited.

Wild Plateau

Deosai National Park

4,114 meters (13,497 ft)

World's 2nd highest plateau. Accessible July to mid-October via high-clearance 4WD jeeps. Extreme cold at night (often drops below 0°C).

All-Weather KKH

Karakoram Highway (KKH)

Islamabad → Gilgit → Hunza

Paved international highway open year-round through Hunza to Sost. Border at Khunjerab Pass (4,693m) operates seasonally for trade/travel.

High-Altitude Medical & Travel Protocols

Essential acclimatization knowledge for safe travel in the Karakoram and Himalayas.

Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS)

Above 2,500m, ascend gradually. Spend 1 night acclimatizing before sleeping at 3,300m+ (Fairy Meadows or Deosai). Drink 3-4 liters of water daily.

Cash Realities

ATMs are available only in Gilgit, Skardu, and Aliabad. Remote valleys (Phander, Shimshal, Baltoro) are strictly cash-only (PKR).

Skardu & Gilgit Flights

PIA mountain flights are subject to visual flight rules. Weather cancellations happen frequently. Never book tight onward international flights.
